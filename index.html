About Northstone

Designed Around the Way You Live

A great kitchen or bathroom is rarely about a single dramatic feature. It is about how the whole space supports your routine.

Thoughtful planning, practical layouts and carefully selected finishes can help homeowners create rooms that work better for everyday life: clear paths to move through, storage within easy reach, lighting that suits each task and materials that are comfortable to live with.

We begin with listening, then translate what we hear into a plan you can review, question and refine before work starts.

The remodeling process

Four clear steps from idea to finished room

Every project is different, but the path is familiar. Timelines depend on scope, materials and scheduling, and are confirmed in writing during planning.

  1. Discover

    We talk through your goals, how you use the space, what is not working and what you would like to keep.

  2. Design & Plan

    Layout options, materials and a written scope are reviewed together so you can make informed decisions.

  3. Remodel

    Work proceeds in a planned sequence, with regular updates on progress and any decisions that need your input.

  4. Final Walkthrough

    We review the finished space with you, note any touch-ups and explain how to care for your new surfaces.

Why planning matters

Why homeowners value good planning

The most satisfying remodels tend to share one thing: decisions were made thoughtfully, in the right order.

  • Clear project goalsKnowing what the space must do makes every later choice easier.
  • Thoughtful material selectionComparing options for look, upkeep and daily use before ordering anything.
  • Better use of spaceLayouts that improve movement, storage and light, not just surfaces.
  • Consistent communicationA shared understanding of scope, decisions and next steps along the way.
