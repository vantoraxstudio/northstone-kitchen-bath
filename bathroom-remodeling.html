Planning your bathroom

Small rooms reward careful decisions

A bathroom packs a lot of function into a compact footprint, so every inch, fixture and finish needs to earn its place.

We look at how the room is used, who uses it and what would make mornings and evenings smoother, then shape the layout, storage and materials around those answers.

Vanities & storage

A vanity that keeps the room calm

The vanity is often the focal point and the main storage in the room. Getting its size, height and storage right helps keep counters clear and everything within easy reach.

  • Drawer organizationShallow and deep drawers for daily items and bulkier supplies.
  • Right-sized surfacesEnough counter space for routines without crowding the room.
  • Mirrors and lightPlaced so faces are evenly lit and the room feels brighter.

Showers & bath areas

Showers and tubs for the way you actually bathe

Some homeowners want a spacious walk-in shower, others a deep soaking tub. We help you decide what suits your household and your room.

  • Comfort and accessEntry height, niches, benches and controls placed for ease of use.
  • Glass and enclosuresOptions that open the room visually while keeping water contained.
  • VentilationGood airflow helps protect finishes and keep the room fresh.

More to consider

Tile, fixtures and layout

Tile & surfaces

Tile sets the character of a bathroom. Size, pattern and finish all affect both look and upkeep.

  • Slip-resistant options for floors
  • Grout choices that are easier to maintain
  • Large formats for fewer joints

Fixtures & lighting

Faucets, shower hardware and lighting are used daily, so comfort and consistency matter.

  • Coordinated metal finishes
  • Layered lighting for different times of day
  • Fixtures that are simple to clean

Layout & functionality

A thoughtful layout can improve flow without enlarging the room.

  • Door swings and walking clearances
  • Storage in niches and wall cabinets
  • Space planning for shared use

Planning process

How a bathroom project unfolds

Timelines depend on scope, materials and scheduling, and are confirmed in writing during planning.

    We talk through how the bathroom is used, who uses it and what you would like to change.

  2. Design & Plan

  3. Remodel

  4. Final Walkthrough

Frequently asked questions

Bathroom remodeling FAQs

Think about who uses the bathroom and how, what you would like to keep or change, and whether the layout will stay the same. Also consider how long the room may be out of use, what storage you need and how much upkeep you are comfortable with. Clear priorities make material and fixture choices much easier.

Wall-mounted or recessed storage, a vanity with deep drawers, a glass shower enclosure and good lighting can all help. Light, consistent colors and larger tiles with fewer joints often make a compact room feel calmer and more open. Door swings and clearances are worth reviewing early.

Compare floor and wall tile, vanity countertops, shower surfaces and fixture finishes. For each, consider water resistance, slip resistance, cleaning needs and how the material looks in your lighting. Ask to see real samples next to each other before deciding.

Helpful details include the approximate size of the bathroom, whether you want a shower, tub or both, any layout changes you are considering, the style you like and photos of the current room. A budget range and desired timing also help. Final pricing and schedules are confirmed only after a full review.
